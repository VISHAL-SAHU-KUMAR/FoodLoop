# FoodLoop 🚜🥗

**FoodLoop** is an **AI-powered Sustainable Food Ecosystem** designed to connect farmers, consumers, restaurants, and NGOs through a smart and transparent platform. Its core mission is to **reduce food waste, empower farmers, and promote sustainable food consumption**.

---

## 🌱 Features

1. **Farm-to-Table Marketplace**  
   Farmers can list seasonal produce directly for consumers, eliminating middlemen and ensuring fair pricing and freshness.

2. **AI Food Waste Prediction**  
   Uses AI models to predict unsold food from restaurants and households and efficiently redistribute it to NGOs or needy beneficiaries.

3. **Blockchain Food Traceability & Rewards**  
   Records every transaction on the blockchain to ensure transparency and rewards consumers with **EcoPoints** redeemable for discounts or donations.

---

## 🎯 Impact

- Reduce food waste by up to 30% in pilot areas  
- Empower farmers with direct market access and fair pricing  
- Increase consumer awareness and engagement in sustainable choices  
- Create a scalable model for sustainable food systems  

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React.js / Next.js (Web), React Native (Mobile)  
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js  
- **Database:** MongoDB / Firebase  
- **AI Module:** Python (TensorFlow / Scikit-learn)  
- **Blockchain:** Ethereum / Polygon  
- **Hosting:** AWS / Firebase Hosting  
- **UI/UX Design:** Figma / Adobe XD
